A vibrant, dynamic musical partnership

Duo Sonus brings together two accordionists renowned for their innovative interpretations of traditional and contemporary music. With a shared passion for world music, our performances seamlessly blend the soulful melodies of tango, classical influences and modern tunes.

Formed in Copenhagen and trained at the Royal Danish Academy of Music, Amalie Ravnsbæk and Romena Lengvinaite have carried their sound from conservatory stages in Helsinki and Kokkola to concert halls, embassies and private celebrations across Denmark and the Baltics.
